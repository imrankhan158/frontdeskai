## Working Hours and Location
SmileCare Dental Clinic is open Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays. Our clinic is located at 204 Linking Road, Bandra West, Mumbai, right next to the metro station.

## Consultation and Treatment Pricing
A standard dental checkup and consultation costs 500 rupees. Teeth cleaning and polishing costs 1,500 rupees. Digital X-rays cost 400 rupees, and root canal treatments start at 4,500 rupees.

## Insurance and Payment Methods
We accept UPI, all major credit and debit cards, and cash. We offer cashless insurance claims for Star Health, HDFC Ergo, and ICICI Lombard dental plans.

## Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy
Appointments can be rescheduled or cancelled free of charge up to 4 hours before the scheduled slot. Late cancellations or no-shows incur a 200-rupee rebooking fee.
